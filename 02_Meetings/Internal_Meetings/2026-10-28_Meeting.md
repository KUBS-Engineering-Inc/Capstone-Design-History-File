# Outline
1. Brief project overview
  - Brief overview of project: capturing volumetric blood flow measurements from Doppler ultrasound recordings
    - User inputs: mp4 Ultrasound recording of baseline or protocol scans
    - Key outputs: perpendicular blood vessel diameter, cross sectional area, doppler (upper) envelope trace
    - Requirements/constraints: PhysioMerge integration, GUI
    - Risk analysis overview: minimal risk, mostly just automation deskilling workers or leading to bias
  - Development process update: mostly planning & iterating on requirements with our stakeholder(s)
    - Dr. Baraa Al-Khazraji: Director of McMaster's Vascular AI Lab
    - Taha Abbasi-Hashemi: PhD student at McMaster's Vascular AI Lab
